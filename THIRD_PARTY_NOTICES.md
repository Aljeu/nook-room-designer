# Third-party assets and licences

Package dependencies retain their respective licences. Install with `npm ci` to inspect the package licence texts in `node_modules/`; do not include that directory in the repository.

## Fonts

- Clash Display: bundled licence at `public/site/fonts/CLASH-LICENSE.txt`.
- Creato Display: bundled SIL Open Font License at `public/site/fonts/CREATO-LICENSE.txt`.


Unused Colitez, Otflag, Strong and Mooxy fonts, along with font-study pages have been removed from the repository folder. Original downloaded font archives and historical visual references are not part of the public source tree.

Homepage screenshots and videos are retained as product media. Confirm ownership and permission for all visuals before publication. No new licence grant for those assets is inferred by this preparation pass.
